EXPLOSION — AN EFFECT, NOT A LAYER

The burn is the real one: a flame front crossing cell by cell at the mixture's own burning velocity, eating hydrogen and oxygen as it goes, with constant-volume pressure rise off the same heat it releases and a compartment that relieves in half a second. Everything drawn on top — the flash, the bloom, the shock ring, the sparks, the smoke, the shake — is effect, and it is tied to a real quantity so it cannot lie about how big the bang was. The soot it leaves is permanent. Switches below turn each effect off so you can judge them one at a time.
