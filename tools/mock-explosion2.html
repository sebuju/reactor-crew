EXPLOSION 2 — ONE BANG, NOT A HUNDRED

The burn underneath is unchanged: a flame front crossing cell by cell at the mixture's own burning velocity, with constant-volume pressure rise off the heat it releases. What changed is who the EFFECT belongs to. In the shipped mockup every burning cell spawned its own ring, its own sparks and its own smoke, so a deflagration read as a hundred small simultaneous detonations laid side by side — every one the same size, because every cell is the same size. Here the burn feeds ONE EVENT: one centre, one radius, one peak pressure, one charge burnt. The fireball, the ring, the debris, the smoke, the flash and the shake are all that event's, sized off it, and the per-cell flame is dimmed to a bed under them. Switch MODE to compare against the old behaviour on the same physics.
